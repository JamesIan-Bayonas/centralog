## 2026-09-28 — Sticker content correction

- **Verification:** Production build passed. Targeted lint still reports the component's existing `any` and data-loading effect errors. ## 2026-09-29 — Narrow phone and enlarged-layout follow-up

- **Screen/files:** `App.css`, `LoginPortal.css`, `AssetDetailSidebar.tsx`/`.css`, `PropertyOverview.css`, `Reports.css`, and `StickerQueueModal.css`.
- **Issues and decision:** The 320px phone layout had no page overflow, but a 240px CSS-viewport stress check exposed clipped menu/utility buttons, login branding and title, and the property print control. Ledger amounts broke mid-number, sticker details were squeezed beside a fixed asset-ID block, asset valuation labels touched their values, and the audit scroll cue was cut off. Shrinking fonts or hiding controls would reduce readability or access. Stacking and wrapping the affected controls and facts was chosen instead. The tradeoff is taller menus and cards; the rendered check found their actions still reachable through page or panel scrolling. On phones up to 360px, the login's secondary explanatory paragraph is hidden so the sign-in form appears sooner; the school identity and purpose remain in its eyebrow and heading.
- **Changes:** Compact navigation utilities stack below 320px; the login header wraps only below 280px and its title wraps safely. Property toolbar buttons stack, asset valuation facts become label/value pairs, sticker ID and details stack, ledger summary decoration is removed to give amounts room, and inventory/audit/ledger sideways-scroll guidance wraps. Print-specific rules remain separate.
- **Boundary:** No form values, submission handlers, API calls, permission predicates, report calculations, sticker membership, print invocations, or procurement/lifecycle transitions changed. The only TSX change adds a styling class to the existing valuation facts container. The transfer dialog was opened and canceled without submitting; inventory selection was cleared afterward.
- **Verification:** Live local administrator views were reviewed at 320px and with a 240px CSS viewport used as a stress proxy, including login, overview, ledger, audit, sticker queue, asset drawer, property page, and transfer dialog. The login was also checked at 360px and in green, white, and night. No page-level horizontal overflow remained in those inspected states; tables and tabs retained their intentional internal scrolling. Production build, five workflow tests, and `git diff --check` passed. Full lint still reports the same 28 errors and 3 warnings from existing effect, `any`, and refresh patterns; no new finding was introduced by this pass.
- **Open:** The in-app browser did not respond to browser-zoom shortcuts and enforces a 240px minimum viewport, so real 200% browser text zoom is **unverified**; the 240px check does not claim to replace it. Short/landscape viewport dialogs and drawers are next. Physical print preview, long-data and table checks, and the separate InventoryStaff role-label issue remain open.
